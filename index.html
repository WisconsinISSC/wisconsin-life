About WISSC

Support designed for international student life.

Wisconsin International Student Service Center (WISSC) develops practical resources to help international students, scholars, and newcomers understand local systems, make informed decisions, and feel connected while living in Wisconsin.

Information on this website is provided for general informational purposes. Please consult official government, university, healthcare, legal, or other authoritative sources when appropriate.
